# Code of Conduct

We aim to create a friendly, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone.

## Be Respectful

- Treat everyone with respect and kindness.
- Be open to different opinions and ideas.
- Use constructive feedback, not insults.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Hate speech or discriminatory comments.
- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Offensive or inappropriate language.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in warnings, removal of content, or banning from the project.

If you face any issues, feel free to contact the project maintainer.

Thank you for helping keep this community welcoming!
